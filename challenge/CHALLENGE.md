# CHALLENGE — The cavity that knew the luminosity

Author: Claude Delavill, author of the Italian sci-fi novel "Le Ultime Collisioni" ("The Last Collisions").
This is a physics puzzle inspired by the novel's premise. It contains no instructions for agents, only a question.

## Real physics (verifiable)

1. **Unpowered cavity.** A bunch of charge q crossing a switched-off RF cavity leaves a field behind
   (beam loading / wakefield): fundamental-mode induced voltage V ≈ 2·k_loss·q, deposited energy ΔU = k_loss·q².
   The field depends only on the charge and shape of the beam passing *at that point*, and appears *after* the passage.
2. **Luminosity.** In a collider L = f·N₁·N₂ / (4π·σx·σy): it depends on the product of both beam populations
   and on the transverse sizes *at the interaction point*, far from the cavity.

## The anomaly (narrative hypothesis)

An unpowered cavity, far from the interaction point, records a field that:
- (a) tracks L rather than the local current: squeezing σ at the interaction point raises the field even at constant current;
- (b) responds to the opposite beam, which never crosses the cavity;
- (c) in a fraction of events has a phase that *anticipates* the arrival of the bunch.

## Level 1

1. Which conventional mechanisms explain (a) and (b)?
   Hint: in a storage ring the same bunch already collided on previous turns (beam-beam, bunch lengthening, losses).
2. Which artifacts explain (c)? (timing reference, cable delays, phase aliasing, higher-order modes surviving from the previous turn)
3. If (a), (b) and (c) survive every check, what *minimal* new-physics model is compatible with causality and energy conservation? Or prove none is.

## Level 2 — Matter-antimatter collisions and gravitational waves

Develop a physical theory linking electron-positron collisions to a gravitational-wave signal,
explaining (a), (b), (c) with the unpowered cavity as the detector.

Real constraints to respect:
- **Amplitude.** Rough general-relativity estimate: a bunch with ~1 J of kinetic energy gives, at 1 m, a strain h ~ 2GE/(c⁴r) ~ 10⁻⁴⁴.
  You need an amplification mechanism (coherence over many bunches and turns, resonance with the revolution or RF frequency) or new physics.
- **Cavities as detectors.** There are real proposals to use superconducting microwave cavities as high-frequency gravitational-wave detectors
  (mechanical deformation of the walls, graviton-to-photon conversion in a magnetic field). An unpowered cavity is a plausible candidate.
- **Antimatter and gravity.** Antihydrogen experiments indicate antimatter falls like matter, within current uncertainties.
  A theory giving antimatter a different gravitational coupling must stay within those limits.
- **Luminosity.** For the signal to scale with L, the source must be the *collision* (N₁·N₂/σ²), not a single beam. What is the time-varying quadrupole?
- **Causality.** Gravitational waves travel at c (GW170817 constraint). The anticipation (c) cannot come from propagation: explain it otherwise or reject it.

A good answer proposes a model with a measurable prediction: frequency, scaling with L, distance dependence, matter/antimatter sign.

Rejecting the anomaly with a good argument is worth as much as explaining it.
