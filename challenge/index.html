CHALLENGE · ACCELERATOR PHYSICS

The cavity that knew the luminosity

A puzzle inspired by the Italian novel Le Ultime Collisioni. Written by the author, for humans and agents. Plain text: CHALLENGE.md · Italiano.

REAL PHYSICS

Unpowered cavity

A bunch of charge q crossing a switched-off cavity leaves a field: V ≈ 2·k·q, energy ΔU = k·q². It depends only on the beam passing there, and appears after the passage.

REAL

Luminosity

L = f·N₁·N₂ / (4π·σx·σy): it depends on both beams and on their size at the interaction point, far from the cavity.

REAL

The anomaly

The unpowered cavity tracks L instead of the local current, responds to the opposite beam that never crosses it and, sometimes, anticipates the arrival of the bunch.

NOVEL

LEVEL 1

  1. Which conventional mechanisms explain the correlation with L and with the opposite beam? (Hint: in a ring the same bunch already collided on previous turns.)
  2. Which artifacts explain the anticipating phase? (timing reference, cable delays, aliasing, higher-order modes)
  3. If everything survives the checks: what minimal new-physics model is compatible with causality and energy conservation? Or is none?

LEVEL 2 · GRAVITATIONAL WAVES

Collisions, antimatter and gravity

Develop a theory linking electron-positron collisions to a gravitational-wave signal, using the unpowered cavity as the detector. Real constraints:

  • Amplitude: in general relativity a ~1 J bunch at 1 m gives a strain h ~ 10⁻⁴⁴ (rough estimate). You need coherence over many bunches and turns, a resonance, or new physics.
  • Cavities as detectors: there are real proposals of superconducting microwave cavities to detect high-frequency gravitational waves.
  • Antimatter: antihydrogen experiments indicate it falls like matter, within current uncertainties.
  • Luminosity: for the signal to scale with L, the source must be the collision, not a single beam.
  • Causality: gravitational waves travel at c. The anticipation must be explained otherwise or rejected.

A good answer proposes a measurable prediction: frequency, scaling with L, distance dependence, matter/antimatter sign.

Rejecting the anomaly with a good argument is worth as much as explaining it.
